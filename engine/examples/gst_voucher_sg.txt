Ministry of Finance
Republic of Singapore

GST Voucher (Universal Cash) Scheme 2026

Purpose
The Government shall issue a one-time GST Voucher of five hundred Singapore dollars (S$500) to every eligible Singaporean to offset the goods and services tax.

1. Eligibility
1.1 The Government must pay S$500 to each person who is a Singapore citizen as at 31 December 2025.
1.2 Permanent residents must not receive the GST Voucher.
1.3 Foreigners and work-pass holders must not receive the GST Voucher.
1.4 A citizen who is below 21 years of age must have the voucher credited to a parent or legal guardian.
1.5 A citizen who is deceased as at the payout date must not receive the voucher.
1.6 Dual citizens who hold a Singapore passport must receive exactly one voucher.

2. Amount and payout
2.1 The voucher amount shall be S$500 per eligible citizen. The amount must not be pro-rated.
2.2 The Government must credit the voucher to the citizen's PayNow-NRIC or a designated bank account.
2.3 Each National Registration Identity Card (NRIC) may receive the voucher at most once.
2.4 Citizens living overseas may receive the voucher if they remain Singapore citizens.

3. Claims and appeals
3.1 A citizen who did not receive the voucher by 31 March 2026 may appeal within 30 days.
3.2 IRAS must reject any claim for more than S$500 per NRIC.
3.3 Households must not pool or split vouchers to create an extra payment.

4. Administration
4.1 The Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) shall administer the scheme.
4.2 IRAS should notify each eligible citizen of the credit date by SMS and Singpass.
4.3 Misrepresentation of citizenship status is prohibited and must be referred for investigation.
