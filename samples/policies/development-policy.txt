Employee Travel and Expense Policy

1. Meals
Employees may claim meals up to SGD 80 per day while travelling for approved business.
A receipt is required for every meal claim.

2. Approval
Claims above SGD 50 require manager approval before reimbursement.
Claims above SGD 200 require finance approval. Finance approval satisfies the manager approval requirement.

3. Accommodation
Hotel claims are allowed up to SGD 220 per night for domestic travel and SGD 300 per night for international travel.

4. Exceptions and unsupported language
The company may make reasonable exceptions in urgent circumstances.
Exceptions must be documented by Finance. Claims that do not follow this policy may be reviewed under applicable law.
