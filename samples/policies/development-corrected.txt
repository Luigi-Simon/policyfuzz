SYNTHETIC HACKATHON SAMPLE — NOT COMPANY POLICY
Synthetic Travel and Expense Policy — Corrected Control

Eligible categories
Meal, hotel, transport, airfare, and incidental expenses are eligible for reimbursement when incurred for business travel.

Category caps
Meal claims have a per-claim cap of SGD 100.
Meal claims have a daily category cap of SGD 100.
Hotel claims have a per-claim cap of SGD 250.
Transport claims have a per-claim cap of SGD 200.

Receipts
Receipts are not required for claims below SGD 50.
Receipts are required for claims at or above SGD 50.

Approvals
Approval is normally not required for a claim.
Hotel claims below SGD 250 require no approval, regardless of destination.
International hotel claims require manager approval and override both the normal no-approval rule and the hotel-specific no-approval rule.
Airfare booked fewer than 14 days before travel requires manager approval.

Unsupported language
Reasonable incidental expenses may be reimbursed.
